Application to Sudokong
- Group the real saved-game count, elapsed time, progress bar and play actions in one rounded card.
- Promote Continue when a game has progress; otherwise promote New Game. Keep the existing disabled Continue state and new-game confirmation.
- Move the app name and utility actions to a compact header. Use the mascot and a subtle board motif as the visual identity.
- Derive all home UI colors from the active game theme. Recolor the mascot and launcher assets to classic blue/slate, retaining natural ivory fur.
- Use a two-column composition on wide screens, smaller artwork on short screens, scrolling for large text, and a brief entrance respecting reduced-motion settings.
- Game rules, persistence, puzzle generation and available routes are unchanged.
